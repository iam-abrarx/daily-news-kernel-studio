# Changelog

Milestones of Daily News Kernel, newest first. Entries describe what changed for the person running the newsroom or watching its videos.

## 2026-11

### 2026-11-12

- Every story now opens on a short branded intro and ends on a sign-off, and the style of each follows the kind of story.
- Stories are classed by kind (tragedy, breaking news, politics, Bangladesh, world, business, technology, science, sport, features). The kind also decides which voice reads the story; six new voices were added for these kinds.
- Compilations open on news highlights, three or four stories to a page, each lighting up as its headline is read, and close on the outlets' own logos.
- Repeat avoidance looks back several days and recognises a retold story even when its wording changes.
- The autopilot moved to a news check every three hours and a compilation every five, with shorter stories inside each compilation.

### 2026-11-11

- **Autopilot.** News checks on the clock, a short for every new story, a chaptered compilation every few hours, delivery to cloud storage in folders by date and time, desktop notifications and a one-click "process all now". It waits when the PC is busy and resumes after a crash or power cut. Its first 35 hours of scheduled running produced 84 stories, 84 shorts and 9 compilations.
- An Autopilot tab in the app to watch and control it.
- A system tray app that starts with the computer and makes quick editions in one click.
- A news team of three original voices, all speaking Bangladeshi English: an anchor, a newsreader and a reporter, with the reader chosen by the story.
- Six music moods chosen to suit each story, each video in its own key, and light sound effects.
- A picture behind every key point, changing as the presenter reaches it, with a dark wash so words stay readable.
- Compilations gained chapters: an on-screen strip showing the story now playing and what comes next, and chapter times in the publish notes.
- End-of-story motion graphics: timelines drawn in calendar order with what is still to come, "why it matters" and quotes read along over pictures, and "what happens next".
- Openly licensed photographs of the people and places a story names, credited with author and licence.
- Video clips are screened for adverts. Two adverts had been saved as news clips; the three delivered videos that used them were remade and replaced.
- Repeated photographs and outlet branding bars are removed from the pictures.
- Each story in a compilation now stays within about a minute and a quarter.

### 2026-11-10

- Cover pictures fixed. Three of 30 saved covers had shown the wrong picture; the next edition had 9 of 9 right.
- Sources, dates and picture credits moved to the final card, with a new end-card design; story cards carry only the story.
- First set of 30-second story videos and a 10.5-minute compilation made from a single edition.
- A frozen web page can no longer stall an edition.
- The app window now appears reliably after a reboot.

### 2026-11-09

- Named **Daily News Kernel**, with its two-tick logo.
- A start window to choose the type of edition (full daily, quick briefing, single topic, deep dive), what to read, what to pick and what to make, before the edition begins.
- Each edition opens on a printed-style front page.
- A restart button for a running edition or the whole app.
- Richer visuals (maps on news cards, footage stills, more sound) tried on a sample story in a separate test copy.

### 2026-11-08

- A timeline editor for re-cutting videos: slide, presenter and music tracks, trims that snap to the beat, undo and redo, and sound that rebuilds itself after each change.
- Faster editions, with the same story choices in 9 of 10 cases.

### 2026-11-07

- First editions. The newsroom gathers stories from news sources, writes them, and checks every line against what was published; stories become slides in three looks and three shapes.
- Motion videos with original music, cut to the beat, opening on the headlines and closing on the sources. A top-stories video is made after every edition.
- Original news presenter voices that read only checked lines.
- One design system shared by the app, the slides and the videos.
- A blind line-by-line review of the writing settings: careful writing had 95% of lines correct and none wrong.
- A check that catches figures stripped of their qualifiers ("at least", "about").
- New Politics and Environment desks and wider Bangladesh coverage, bringing the newsroom to 64 sources across nine desks.
- Pages that load the next story while the reader scrolls no longer mix two stories into one article.
- A layout checker over every slide and video shot; text that broke differently in video than on the slide went from 57 of 963 shots to none.
